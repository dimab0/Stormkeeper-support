Privacy · effective October 20, 2026

No data leaves the station.

Stormkeeper Light has no server of its own, no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. (This replaces the policy of October 15, 2026; nothing changed in substance.)

On the device

Your best score per night, which nights you have survived, and your settings. Deleting the app deletes them.

Game Center

If you are signed in, your best per night and your total go to Game Center leaderboards under your Game Center name, under Apple's privacy policy. Signing out still lets you play everything.

The Full Watch

Sold by the App Store. Apple handles payment; the game only learns that you own it. We never see your name, email or payment details.

Children · changes · contact

Nothing is collected from anyone. A changed policy will be posted here with a new date. braznichenko@gmail.com
