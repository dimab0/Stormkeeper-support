Arcade · iPhone and iPad

One lighthouse. Twelve storm nights. Your thumb on the beam.

Ships come out of the dark running blind for the rocks. Drag anywhere on the sea to swing the beam, hold each ship in the light until its rescue ring fills, and keep the lamp alive until dawn.

Focus

Hold your thumb still for a narrow, stronger beam. It heats the lens and drains the battery.

Pulse

A flash all round that helps every ship near the tower and shows the hidden rocks. Then it has to recharge.

Weather

Fog shortens the beam and hides boats (watch for the horn marks). Squalls make the lamp flicker and push ships off course.

Chains and dawn

Rescue back to back for up to x5. Lose three ships and the light has failed. Every night has its own Game Center ranking.

Price

Nights 1 to 3 are free. The Full Watch, one in-app purchase, opens nights 4 to 12.
