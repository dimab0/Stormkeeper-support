How to play

Drag anywhere on the sea to turn the beam. Keep a ship in the light until the ring round it fills: it turns for the harbor and scores. Hold your thumb still to focus the beam (brighter and faster, but it heats the lens and uses power). Tap Pulse, or double-tap the sea, for a flash that helps every ship near the tower and shows hidden rocks. Three ships lost ends the night; keep the light until dawn to open the next one.

The purchase

Nights 1–3 are free. The Full Watch ($2.99, one time) opens nights 4–12. If you bought it before, use Restore purchase on the purchase screen or in Settings, signed in with the same Apple Account.

Leaderboards

Sign in to Game Center in your device’s Settings to appear on the leaderboards. Your best scores are kept on the device when you are not signed in and are posted the next time a night ends while you are.

Comfort

Settings has Reduced Flash (no bright flashes from the pulse or lightning), haptics and sound switches, and a left-handed layout.
