Playing

  1. Drag anywhere on the sea; the beam turns toward your finger.
  2. Keep a ship in the beam until its ring fills. It turns for harbour and scores.
  3. Hold still to focus; tap PULSE (or double-tap the sea) for a flash all round.
  4. Watch the Power and Lens Heat gauges. A flat battery means a short blackout.

The Full Watch

Opens nights 4 to 12. Bought it before? Tap Restore on the Full Watch page or under Station, signed in with the same Apple Account.

Ranks

Rankings use Game Center (Settings › Game Center). Bests made while signed out stay on the device and are posted later.

Comfort

Under Station: reduced flash (no lightning or shake), haptics off, sound off, and the Pulse button on the left for left-handed play.

A night feels too hard

Each night opens when the one before is survived. Try focusing less: the lens cools and the battery recovers when you lift your thumb.
